Why OpenSuite

Autonomy, by design

Most collaboration tools rent you access to your own data. OpenSuite gives it back.

🔐

Secure & sovereign

Run everything on your own hardware. Stay in full control of your critical data and meet your compliance obligations.

🧩

Flexible & modular

Enable only the apps you need and integrate with your existing identity and document systems. No rip-and-replace.

🌍

Open by default

Built on open-source components and open standards. Inspect it, extend it, contribute to it — no lock-in, ever.

Open source

Standing on the shoulders of public code

OpenSuite builds on a thriving ecosystem of sovereign-workplace projects — including the Netherlands' MijnBureau, France's La Suite Numérique and Germany's openDesk — so you benefit from work done in the open, by many.

🔌

Single sign-on

One identity across every app via OpenID Connect, with token exchange between services.

☸️

Cloud-native deploy

Ship the whole suite to Kubernetes with Helm. Reproducible, scalable and easy to operate.

🛡️

Built-in policy & scanning

Automated security policies and continuous scanning baked into the platform.
