Everything a team uses every day

Eight apps that are really one.

They share one design, one account and one search, so moving from an email to a document to a call never feels like changing products.

Mail

Conversations, folders, search, attachments and push delivery, on your own domain.

Calendar

Day, week, month and agenda views, repeating events, invitations, colleagues' free and busy times.

Chat

Direct and group conversations with files, images, unread counts and a one-click call.

Meet

Permanent room links, guests without accounts, screen sharing, background blur and virtual backgrounds.

Files

Folders, previews, trash, and sharing with colleagues or by public link.

Docs

Live co-editing, comments, suggestions, version history, and Word import and export.

Sheets

Formulas, formatting, charts, several sheets and live cursors. Opens and saves Excel files.

Slides

Layouts, themes, speaker notes and a presenter view. Opens and saves PowerPoint files.

Search and admin

One box finds people, mail, files, documents and their contents. Admins manage people and domains in the same app.

Fast on purpose

No spinners. No waiting for the server.

Your mail, calendar and documents are kept on your device and drawn straight away, then brought up to date in the background. Documents keep working offline and sync when you reconnect.
