Why we built Open Suite

Mijn Bureau had the right idea. Using it showed us what was missing.

A portal joining strong European open-source tools was a compelling start. But assembling applications is not the same as making a product teams can trust.

The promise was better than the experience.

Our first Mijn Bureau deployment felt fragmented: different brands and navigation, no integrated Mail, confusing dead ends, and setup that still demanded an integration project. Users saw the seams. Operators had to understand all of them.

So we turned the reference stack into a distribution that behaves like one product.

One identity and one visual languageA shared header, consistent Open Suite branding, and SSO across the workplace.
Journeys that finish properlyDurable links, useful close and back behavior, connected calendar-to-meeting flows, and no disposable error-screen dead ends.
Integration work shipped, not documented as homeworkImages, patches, configuration, networking, certificates, and app wiring arrive as one reproducible deployment.
The stack disappearsPeople use Mail, Files, Docs, and Meet. They do not need to know which upstream project owns each screen.

The promise is deliberately boring: sign in and get to work. It just works.
